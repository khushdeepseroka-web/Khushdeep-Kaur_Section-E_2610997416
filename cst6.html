Famous Places in India

Explore some of the most beautiful and historical places of India.

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is one of India's most famous monuments. It was built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. It is famous for its beautiful white marble architecture.

Visit Incredible India

2. Red Fort, Delhi

The Red Fort is a historic fort located in Delhi. It was constructed by Shah Jahan and is an important symbol of Indian history.

Learn More

3. Gateway of India, Mumbai

The Gateway of India is a famous monument situated in Mumbai. It overlooks the Arabian Sea and is one of the most popular tourist attractions in the city.

Visit Incredible India

4. Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Hawa Mahal, also known as the Palace of Winds, is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It is famous for its unique architecture and hundreds of small windows.

Rajasthan Tourism

5. Golden Temple, Amritsar

The Golden Temple is located in Amritsar, Punjab. It is one of the most important religious and tourist destinations in India. The temple is surrounded by a beautiful sacred pool.
