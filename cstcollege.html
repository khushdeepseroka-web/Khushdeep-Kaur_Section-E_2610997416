MY COLLEGE:CHITKARA UNIVERSITY

ABOUT COLLEGE

Chitkar University,located in Rajpura, Punjab, is a premier private institution recognized as a leading educational hub in North India. Established in 2010 under the Chitkara University Act, the sprawling 70-acre campus sits along the Chandigarh-Patiala National Highway. It holds a prestigious NAAC A+ accredition and is highly regarded for its focus on multi-disciplinary,industry-relevant learning across fields like engineering,management,pharmacy,and health sciences. Known for its robust corporate connections,state-of-the-art infrastructure,and strong placement records with top global recruiters, the university emphasizes research-driven academic excellence and international exposure through various global pathway programs.
